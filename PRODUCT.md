# Product requirements

## Audience and problem

People in the United States affected by cancer may need to coordinate travel, lodging, and expenses around a treatment-related appointment. Harborstep helps a patient or caregiver make a practical plan and find the appropriate organization to contact. It is not a booking service or a clinical decision tool.

## Core journey

1. Enter a visit label, date, city, and state, and select transportation, lodging, and/or financial support.
2. Review source-linked organizations relevant to the selected needs. Availability, eligibility, and funding must be confirmed with the provider.
3. Add, edit, complete, or remove personal next steps; record estimated costs.
4. Return on the same browser or print the plan for a trusted person.

## Acceptance criteria

- Required fields display actionable validation errors and preserve entered values.
- The plan can be edited and survives a reload in the same browser when storage is available.
- Resources reflect selected support categories; no location-specific availability is inferred.
- Costs reflect user-entered estimates and are never presented as a quote or guaranteed assistance.
- The plan can be printed and deleted, and the storage limitation is visible.
- No diagnosis, treatment recommendation, eligibility determination, automatic application, or booking is made.

## Evaluation and limits

Verify the flow in a browser on desktop and mobile, including validation, task edits, deletion, storage failure, print, and keyboard navigation. Conduct patient and navigator usability sessions before claiming measured benefit or broad readiness. U.S. resources are a small curated set, not a complete directory.
