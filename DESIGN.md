# Harborstep design direction

Audience: U.S. cancer patients and caregivers arranging the practical details of an appointment. The primary job is to make an achievable, user-controlled logistics plan.

Use a clear, airy light canvas (#f3f7f8), deep blue ink (#183c4b), calm sea-glass (#dcece9), white surfaces (#ffffff), and restrained coral (#d16e53) only for meaningful emphasis. The look is contemporary and tactile, using translucency for the navigation bar and focused cards rather than behind dense content.

Typography is system sans-serif for readability, with a distinctive soft serif used sparingly in the main headline. Navigation is simple and explicit. Touch targets are generous; labels and contrast carry state without relying on color. Make changing or deleting user-entered data clear. Respect reduced motion. Use a single-column reading order on mobile.

Resource cards always link to official provider pages and state that availability and eligibility are determined by providers, not the application. Nothing in the interface claims to book a ride, reserve lodging, diagnose, provide clinical advice, or handle an application on a user's behalf.
